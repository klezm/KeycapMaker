Naming note:
Unlike the bundled NotoSans-Variable.ttf, whose nameID 1 is the plain family
"Noto Sans", this font's nameID 1 carries the default instance's weight
("Noto Sans Hebrew Thin") and the plain family lives in nameID 16. The bundled
OpenSCAD runtime resolves the `Noto Sans Hebrew` query through the typographic
family, and an unqualified query renders at Regular rather than at the Thin
default instance, so the app's usual `<family>:style=<Style>` font queries
behave the same as they do for the other bundled variable fonts.

Script coverage:
All 27 Hebrew letters including the five final forms (ך ם ן ף ץ) are present,
which is what the ISO German + Hebrew keyset legends require.

Use note:
The bundled font is used unmodified as a legend font asset and redistributed
with its OFL text.
